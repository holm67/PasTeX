\section{Citations and notes}
Open References, Bibliography to change databases, backend and style.
Choose an installed style, such as numeric, authoryear or apa. Refresh the
preview after changing it. Citation entries can be clicked to edit their
keys and optional notes.

A parenthetical citation is \parencite{knuth1984}; a narrative citation is
\textcite{lamport1994}. Notes are supported:
\parencite[see][123]{knuth1984}. Several entries can be cited together:
\parencite{knuth1984,lamport1994}.

Select its text to copy it; Ctrl+click a link to open its destination.
