Open this master in PasTeX to edit its linked files in place. Click
\texttt{[hide]} or \texttt{[show]} on a separator to fold a file;
Ctrl+click its name to open it separately. Saving keeps the original files.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Command & Page breaks & Child compiles alone? \\
\midrule
\texttt{\textbackslash input} & None & Body fragment \\
\texttt{\textbackslash include} & Before and after & Body fragment \\
\texttt{\textbackslash subfile} & None & Yes, with the subfiles class \\
\texttt{\textbackslash subfileinclude} & Before and after & Yes, with the subfiles class \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\verb|\includeonly| filters included chapters; ordinary inputs and subfiles
are still read. The Methods chapter also contains nested subfiles in
\texttt{details/}; expand or fold each level independently.
